# MediSwift 💊

### Fast. Local. Safe. Smart.

**MediSwift** is a hyperlocal medicine delivery platform that connects customers with verified local pharmacies and delivery partners for fast, safe, and reliable medicine access. Users can search medicines, check availability, upload prescriptions, add medicines to cart, place orders, and track deliveries. Pharmacies can manage inventory, prescriptions, orders, and delivery requests, while delivery partners handle secure order delivery. MediSwift also includes AI-assisted prescription processing, medication safety checks for potential drug interactions, duplicate medicines and allergies, ADR reporting, and temperature-sensitive delivery workflows. The platform follows a pharmacist-in-the-loop approach, keeping healthcare professionals involved in important medication and prescription decisions.


## 🚀 Features

### 👤 Customer Portal
- User registration and login
- Search medicines
- Find nearby pharmacies
- View medicine availability
- Add medicines to cart
- Checkout and place orders
- Upload prescriptions
- Track orders and deliveries
- Report suspected ADRs
- Medication safety alerts

### 🏪 Pharmacy Portal
- Pharmacy login and management
- Add and manage medicines
- Inventory and stock management
- Receive customer orders
- View and verify prescriptions
- Approve/reject prescriptions
- Manage delivery requests
- Manage pharmacy information

### 🚚 Delivery Portal
- Delivery partner login
- View delivery requests
- Accept/reject delivery requests
- Pickup and delivery status
- Current delivery management
- Delivery history

### 🛡️ Admin Portal
- Customer management
- Pharmacy verification
- Delivery partner management
- Order management
- Prescription monitoring
- Medication safety alerts
- ADR report management
- Delivery monitoring
- Revenue overview

## 🧠 Smart Safety Features

MediSwift follows a pharmacist-in-the-loop approach.

The system can assist with:

- Prescription information extraction
- Drug interaction alerts
- Duplicate medicine alerts
- Allergy alerts
- ADR reporting
- Temperature-sensitive delivery workflows

AI is used as an assistance tool and does not replace pharmacists, doctors, or other qualified healthcare professionals.
